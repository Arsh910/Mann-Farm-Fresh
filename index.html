    FAQ

    Got questions?

    Short answers to what buyers ask us most.

    What does contract farming mean for me?

    We agree with our farmers what to grow and when to harvest. So the size, ripeness and quality you buy are planned months ahead.

    Why don't you show prices?

    Prices depend on grade, quantity and route. Every quote shows the unit price, delivery charge and GST, and you can lock a weekly rate on core items.

    What if something is bad on arrival?

    Check it with our driver at the door. Anything below your grade comes off the invoice or is replaced on the next run.

    What if an item is short?

    We tell you the evening before and offer a substitute. Nothing is swapped without your approval.

    Do you give credit?

    Regular accounts can move to weekly or monthly billing after the first month. Every order comes with a GST invoice.

    Can I order on WhatsApp?

    Yes. Send your list to +91 98721 78832 before the cut-off for your route.
