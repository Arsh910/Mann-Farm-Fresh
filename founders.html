The people behind your produce

One founder has spent 20 years in the field, the other 15 years building software. Together they run the farms, the supply chain and the systems that get produce to your door.

HM Agri-supply chain expert

Chief Executive Officer

Harpreet Singh Mann

With over 20 years of hands-on experience in farming, sourcing and selling fresh produce, Harpreet is the backbone of Mann Farm Fresh's supply chain. He has built long-standing relationships with farmers, markets and food outlets across major cities in North India, earning a reputation for consistent quality and trustworthy service.

Having grown up close to the soil, he brings a deep understanding of how real freshness looks, smells and tastes. From selecting seeds to judging ripeness, his expertise makes sure every item Mann Farm Fresh delivers meets the highest standards.

Today he leads all farming, procurement and quality-check operations, so that every vegetable and fruit is harvested at dawn and reaches customers at peak freshness.

BJ Technology strategist

Chief Technology Officer

Baljeet Singh Jaura

A seasoned solution software architect with over 15 years of industry experience, Baljeet brings the technological strength that powers Mann Farm Fresh. Having built digital systems for diverse industries, he is passionate about creating solutions that simplify everyday life.

Rooted in a middle-class upbringing where fresh morning produce was a family ritual, he envisioned a way to blend tradition with technology. His goal: make farm-fresh vegetables accessible with just a few taps.

At Mann Farm Fresh he leads all things tech, from ordering and user experience to backend systems and delivery optimisation, keeping the platform simple, intuitive and reliable.
